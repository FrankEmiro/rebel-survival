2. What it is for

Rebel Survival is meant to help you prepare and find your bearings. It collects and summarises information from several sources, but does not guarantee that it is complete, correct or up to date: it may contain errors or outdated indications. It is for information only and the calculations are estimates. It does not replace the instructions of Civil Protection, health authorities or other bodies, nor a doctor's advice.

Always check information against official sources. In an emergency always follow the instructions of the authorities and call 112.

You decide what to do and you must be aware of the risks of the activities described (for example use of fire, unsafe water, medicines, food, travel). If in doubt, don't do it.

3. "Caution" guides and local rules

Guides labelled "Caution" contain indications not yet verified by an official source: if applied wrongly they can be dangerous. Use them only as a guide.

Some of the activities described (for example fishing, gathering plants, lighting fires) are regulated by law: follow the rules of the place where you are.

Features marked "experimental" (for example Bluetooth chat and AI answers) may not work on every phone: do not rely on them alone.
