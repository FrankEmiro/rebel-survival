Privacy notice

Rebel Survival · last updated: 17 November 2026

This is an English translation of the Italian text. If the two differ, the Italian text prevails.

In short

You have no account and we do not ask you for data. What you write in the app stays on your phone. Some optional features download files from the internet: at that moment the server sending them sees your IP address, as with any website.

1. Who is the controller

FrankEmiro, author of the app (github.com/FrankEmiro). For questions or requests, open an issue in the project's issues.

2. The data you enter

Profile, supplies, plan, lists, useful numbers and chat messages stay only on your device. No account, no profiling cookies, no statistics: this data does not reach us. You can delete it whenever you like by clearing the app's data in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. When the app uses the internet

  • Android app (GitHub). The app is downloaded and updated only from GitHub: we do not have a server of our own. When you download it or when it checks for updates, GitHub receives your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Maps (OpenStreetMap). Only if you use or download the map, the images come from the servers of the OpenStreetMap Foundation, which receive your IP address. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Offline Wikipedia (Kiwix). Only if you download parts of Wikipedia, the files come from Kiwix servers (download.kiwix.org), which receive your IP address. After the download you read them without a network.
  • AI-written answers (optional). Only if you turn them on in "Ask", the device downloads the program and the AI model once from jsDelivr, Hugging Face and GitHub, which receive your IP address. After that the AI runs on the phone: questions and answers are not sent to anyone.

Fonts and icons are inside the app: they are not downloaded from external services.

4. Chat between nearby phones (Android app)

The chat works over Bluetooth, without internet and without going through any server of ours. When it is on, nearby phones with the chat see the name you have chosen and can receive your public messages, also by passing through other phones. Private messages are encrypted. You can also send photos, voice messages and small files: the same rules apply, and if you send them to "Everyone nearby" anyone who is nearby can see them. Messages, photos and files stay only on the phones that receive them.

5. Android app permissions

  • Location: to show you where you are on the map and compass. The location stays on the phone.
  • Camera: to read QR codes and for the flashlight.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): for the chat between nearby phones.
  • Microphone: only if you record a voice message in the chat between nearby phones. Audio is recorded when you tap "Voice" and is sent only if you confirm.
  • Notifications: to show the progress of downloads that continue in the background.
  • Internet: for the features in point 3.

You can deny or remove any permission in your phone settings: the related feature stops working, the rest of the app does not.

6. Legal basis and rights

The technical data in point 3 is needed to make the service work for you (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679). You can ask for access, erasure or object (Arts. 15–22) through the contact in point 1, and lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
